Install on iPhone / iPad

iOS requires adding the web app manually through Safari:

1

Tap the Share icon at the bottom (or top) of your Safari browser bar.

2

Scroll down the share menu options and select Add to Home Screen .

3

Tap Add in the top right corner. The LGBTQ Moray Health Hub icon will appear on your Home Screen!
